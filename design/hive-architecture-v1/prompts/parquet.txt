Lighting/mood: even quiet diffuse illumination, no directional light, no brilliant highlights, no dark corners or vignette. Midtone surface that lets tiny residents and furniture read above it.
Constraints: square format, truly seamless wrap on both axes, fully opaque to every edge. Surface texture only. No rugs, objects, props, lettering, symbols, logos, borders, frames, room scene, perspective, bevelled tile perimeter, cast shadows, gradients, shiny varnish, giant boards, or checkerboard background.
