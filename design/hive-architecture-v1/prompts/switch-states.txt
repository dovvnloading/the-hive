Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production pixel-art sprite sheet for two physical wall light-switch states in a cozy handmade RPG workshop.
Primary request: Create exactly TWO matching small wall light switches, arranged side by side in one horizontal row, on a genuinely transparent PNG alpha background. LEFT is ON: ivory ceramic mechanical toggle tilted UP, with a tiny amber indicator lit within the plate. RIGHT is OFF: the same ivory ceramic toggle tilted DOWN, with that same indicator dark and unlit. The two plates must be identical in size, design, proportions, materials, front view, and lighting; ONLY the lever position and the indicator state differ.
Subject/materials: each switch is a simple vertical aged-brass outer plate, four tiny corner screws, and a dark forest-green enamel inset with an ivory ceramic mechanical toggle. Warm walnut-and-brass workshop palette; compact handmade hardware. No surrounding walnut block: the brass plate is the entire outer silhouette.
Style/medium: actual crisp pixel art with bold deliberate pixel clusters, stepped edges, restrained tiny highlights, simple clean readable shapes. These switches will be rendered at just 6 by 10 logical game pixels each, so prioritize a clear ivory up/down lever silhouette, a brass rectangular outline, and one tiny indicator. Minimal texture; no fine grunge, ornamental flourishes, tiny lettering, or photographic material grain.
Composition/framing: straight-on front orthographic view, no perspective and no visible side faces. Each plate has a 2:3 width-to-height aspect ratio. Both plates level and equal-sized, with generous truly transparent outer margins and a wide transparent gutter between them. Suggested landscape canvas 1536 by 1024, with each individual plate about 320 by 480 pixels. Keep both plates completely visible.
Transparency: genuine alpha transparency outside the two solid switch-plate silhouettes. All surrounding pixels and the gutter must be fully transparent, with no drawn backdrop or checkerboard. Solid plate interiors. No cast shadow, wall, external halo, or glow outside a plate.
Constraints: exactly two individual plates, one left ON/up and one right OFF/down, same design except state, no connecting element, no state labels.
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, labels, icons, ON/OFF words, wall surface, room, frame around the sheet, furniture, exposed wiring, cables, holes through the plate, extra switches, alternate views, photorealism, vector art, antialiased blurry painting, heavy weathering, luminous aura, shadow outside the plate.
