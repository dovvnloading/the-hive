Use case: stylized-concept

It must be cohesive with mellow amber oak and walnut parquet, forest-green furniture and copper/brass trim.
Pattern and scale: quiet staggered arrangement of irregular hand-cut rectangular and square pavers, largely rectilinear joints and a small range of sizes. Imagine this whole square displayed at 96 by 96 logical game pixels: about six to eight medium-small pavers across. Narrow dark desaturated olive-charcoal grout, gentle irregularities, occasional tiny worn corner.
Color palette: warm grey-green slate, muted sage and olive-grey, slight blue-grey mineral variation, some subdued old-honey limestone accents. Have more specific rich material personality than generic grey stone while keeping contrast moderate. The accents are pavers within the same pattern, never symbols or decorative motifs.
Materials/textures: delicate sparse mineral wear made of purposeful few-pixel clusters, tiny chips and quiet mottling; each stone mostly broad calm midtones. No bright speckle noise.
Lighting/mood: flat even diffuse illumination, no directional light, no brilliant highlights, no dark corners or vignette. A readable quiet background for tiny walking residents.
Constraints: square format, truly seamless wrap on both axes, fully opaque to every edge. Surface texture only. No symbols, lettering, runes, text, rugs, objects, plants, props, borders, frames, room scene, perspective, bevelled tile perimeter, cast shadows, gradients, shiny wet surfaces, or checkerboard background.
